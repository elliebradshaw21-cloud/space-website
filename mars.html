Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and the second smallest planet in the solar system. Named after the Roman god of war, Mars is often called the "Red Planet" because its surface is covered in rusty, iron-rich dust. It has a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide. Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the biggest volcano in the solar system. It's about three times taller than Mount Everest!

Mars Facts
Diameter 6,792 km
Mass 6.39 × 1023 kg (0.11 Earths)
Moons 2 (Phobos and Deimos)
Orbit Distance 227,943,824 km (1.52 AU)
Orbit Period 687 days
Surface Temperature -153°C to 20°C
