Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the rocky planets. It is the only planet in our solar system not named after a Greek or Roman god. Earth formed about 4.54 billion years ago and is the only planet we know of that has life. About 71% of its surface is covered in water, which is why it looks so blue from space!

Earth Facts
Diameter 12,742 km
Mass 5.97 × 1024 kg
Moons 1
Orbit Distance 149,598,262 km (1 AU)

Surface Temperature -89°C to 57°C
