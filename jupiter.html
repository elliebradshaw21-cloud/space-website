Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the solar system. It is two and a half times more massive than all the other planets combined! It is made mostly of hydrogen and helium gas, which is why it's called a "gas giant". Jupiter's famous Great Red Spot is a giant storm that is wider than the whole Earth and has been raging for hundreds of years.

Jupiter Facts
Diameter 142,984 km
Mass 1.90 × 1027 kg (318 Earths)
Moons 115
Rings 4
Orbit Distance 778,340,821 km (5.20 AU)
Orbit Period 4,333 days (about 12 years)
Temperature (cloud tops) -108°C
